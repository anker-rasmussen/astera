# Astera Privacy Policy

_Version 1.1, May 2026._

This is the same policy that ships inside the app (Settings → The promise → Read the full promise) and that is published at [anker-rasmussen.github.io/astera/privacy](https://anker-rasmussen.github.io/astera/privacy/). All three say the same thing, at the same version. If they ever disagree, that is a bug: please file an issue.

## Astera collects nothing.

There is no analytics service in this app. There is no advertising. There are no third-party SDKs of any kind: not Facebook's, not Mixpanel's, not Amplitude's, not Google's, not Segment's. The developer who built Astera cannot read your data, because the developer has no server to read it from.

## What this means on the App Store

Apple asks every developer to declare which categories of data their app collects. Astera's declaration is "Data Not Collected" across every category Apple lists: Contact Info, Health & Fitness, Financial Info, Location, Sensitive Info, Identifiers, Usage Data, Diagnostics, Search History, Browsing History, User Content, and the rest. Nothing leaves your device for us. Nothing is associated with you for us. Nothing is used to track you across other apps or websites.

## Where your data lives

Everything you log in Astera (your cycles, your symptoms, your notes, your settings) is stored on this phone. If you are signed into iCloud, Astera also keeps an encrypted copy in your own iCloud, so it syncs to your other Apple devices. Apple's iCloud encryption protects it in transit and at rest. If you turn on Advanced Data Protection in your Apple ID settings, not even Apple can read it. The Astera developer cannot, in any configuration, read your iCloud data.

## Apple Health

If you turn on the Apple Health toggle, Astera reads menstrual flow samples written by other apps you've used (so you don't lose your history when switching to Astera) and writes the flow you log here back into Apple Health. We comply with Apple's HealthKit terms: we do not use any data obtained through HealthKit for advertising, marketing, or other use-based data mining, and we do not share it with third parties. We can't share it anyway, because we never receive it. It stays on your device.

## Calendar

If you turn on the Calendar toggle, Astera writes predicted period dates to a dedicated "Astera" calendar that lives on your device. It never reads, touches, or modifies your other calendars.

## Notifications

Any reminders Astera sends (period in three days, day-of, and so on) are scheduled locally by your iPhone. There is no push-notification server. No remote system knows when your reminders fire.

## Astera+

If you choose to support the project with an optional in-app purchase, Apple processes the transaction under Apple's own terms. We receive no payment details, no card number, no name. Apple shares aggregate sales reports with us (counts only, no identification of individuals).

## What we don't do

We don't sell anything to anyone. We don't share with anyone. We don't have ad partners. We don't have "marketing partners". We don't keep aggregated statistics. We don't measure your engagement. We don't A/B test your retention. We don't fingerprint your device. We don't track you across other apps. If Astera ever does any of these things, it has broken its promise and you have every right to leave.

## Your rights

You can export every piece of data you've logged at any time. Settings → Your data → Take a copy with you. It saves a readable PDF you can open in any reader, print, or send to a clinician.

You can delete every piece of data you've logged at any time. Settings → Your data → Delete everything. It wipes the local copy on this phone and the encrypted iCloud copy. There's no recovery. That's by design.

You can change everything you told us during setup. Settings → About you. Pronouns, cycle state, birth year, the greeting we use for you, anything.

## Children and teens

Astera is intended for users who are 13 or older. If a user enters a birth year that suggests they're under 18, Astera quietly hides fertility and pregnancy-related features. Sexual activity logging is hidden for users under 16. If a user enters a birth year below 9, Astera shows a gentle note suggesting a trusted adult is a better resource than an app for the earliest years. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, regardless of age, because we do not collect data at all.

## The legal small print

Under GDPR, CCPA, UK GDPR, and similar laws: your "right to access" is the Export button. Your "right to erasure" is the Delete button. Your "right to data portability" is the PDF format itself. Your "right to rectification" is the in-app editing. Astera satisfies these rights by architecture, not by policy. The developer never holds your health data, so there is nothing to hand over and nothing to forget.

Apple acts as the technical processor for iCloud, HealthKit, EventKit, and in-app purchases. Their privacy posture (under Advanced Data Protection in particular) is the strongest in the industry for this data class. You can read Apple's own commitments at [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Astera makes no medical claims. Predictions are estimates, not advice. If something about your cycle worries you, please talk to a clinician.
