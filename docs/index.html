Astera

The tracker you'd build for your sister.

Privacy-first iOS period and cycle tracking. No backend, no third-party SDKs, no analytics. Your data stays on your phone and in your own iCloud, encrypted by Apple, invisible to us.

What's in it

Honest predictions with confidence ranges, not point estimates. Mode-aware cycles for regular, irregular, PCOS, IUD, perimenopause, TTC, pregnant, post-loss, postpartum, on testosterone, and surgical menopause. Per-symptom severity. Cravings and lifestyle tracking, opt-in. Apple Health and Calendar integration, opt-in. Face ID lock, opt-in. Import history from any app that writes to Apple Health.

What's not in it

No accounts. No advertising. No engagement metrics. No social features. No witchcraft. No upsells. The tracker is free.
